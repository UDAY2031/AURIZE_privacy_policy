System Logs: Privacy Protocol

AURIZE Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 30, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Welcome to AURIZE ("the App", "we", "us"). AURIZE is a fitness RPG system designed to help Players track their physical evolution. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App on Android or iOS, why we collect it, who it is shared with, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

In short: your camera video never leaves your device, we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and you can delete your account and data from inside the App at any time.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address and password: used to create and sign in to your account through Firebase Authentication. Your password is handled by Firebase and is never visible to us.
  • Username: the Player name you choose.

1.2 Profile Picture (Optional)

  • If you choose to set one, the photo you pick from your photo library is uploaded and stored in Firebase Storage so it can appear on your profile, Hunter ID card and leaderboard entry.
  • We only access the single photo you select. We do not scan or upload anything else from your photo library.

1.3 Camera (Exercise Tracking)

AURIZE uses your device's camera for AI Exercise Tracking (push-ups, squats and sit-ups) in daily quests, duels and raids.

  • Purpose: on-device computer vision detects body position to count your repetitions and check your form.
  • Storage: we do not record, store or transmit any video or images. All processing happens locally, in real time, on your device. Only the resulting rep count is saved.

1.4 Location

AURIZE uses your device's precise location (GPS) for the Running Tracker and the Nearby Leaderboard.

  • Running quests: while a running quest is in progress, the App reads your location to measure distance and pace. This continues in the background during that run (for example, with your phone locked in your pocket) so the run is measured correctly. Your device shows a location indicator while this is happening. Tracking stops as soon as you leave the run screen. Your route is not stored; only the total distance is saved.
  • Nearby Leaderboard: when you open the Nearby leaderboard, your current coordinates are saved to your profile. Other signed-in Players' apps use these saved coordinates to work out who is within range of them. The App only displays that a Player is nearby; it never displays anyone's location. Your saved coordinates are updated each time you open the Nearby leaderboard and are removed when you delete your account.
  • Location is never used for advertising and is never collected when you are not running a quest or viewing the Nearby leaderboard.

1.5 Motion & Activity

  • During running quests, the App reads your device's step counter and motion activity (for example, whether you appear to be walking, running or in a vehicle). This is used to count distance on a treadmill or when GPS is weak, and to reject runs recorded from a vehicle.
  • This data is processed on your device. Only the run result is saved.

1.6 Game Progress

To sync your progress across sessions and devices, we store your level, rank, XP, health, Dungeon Points, Room Cards, name tags, streaks, quest history, raid and duel results, and daily limits.

1.7 Social Features

  • Friends, friend requests and blocks you create.
  • Duel challenges and duels: who you dueled, the exercises and the scores.
  • Chat messages: messages you send to friends or duel opponents. Chats are temporary by design (see Section 5). Offensive words are automatically masked.
  • Reports: if you report a message, the report (including the message and the Players involved) is kept for moderation.
  • Online status: whether you are currently online, shown to your friends.

1.8 Notifications

  • If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token for your device so we can send you duel challenges, friend requests and similar alerts.
  • Daily quest reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

1.9 Purchases

  • Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your card or payment details.
  • We receive and store a purchase token or transaction ID, the product purchased, and (for subscriptions) its status and expiry date, so we can verify the purchase with the store and grant what you bought.

1.10 Feedback & Technical Logs

  • If you send feedback from the App, we store your message with your account ID so we can follow up.
  • Our servers keep technical logs (such as errors and request times) to find bugs and keep the System stable. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's features: tracking workouts, syncing progress, leaderboards, duels, friends and chat.
  • To verify purchases and keep the game fair (for example, validating reps and runs and preventing cheating).
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To moderate chat and respond to reports, feedback and support requests.
  • To fix bugs and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. What Other Players Can See

  • Your username, profile picture, level, rank, name tag and leaderboard position.
  • Your online status (friends only).
  • Your duel scores, to the opponents you duel.
  • Your chat messages, to the Players you send them to.
  • On the Nearby leaderboard, Players near you can see that you are nearby. The App does not display your location, but it does use your last saved coordinates to calculate this (see Section 1.4).

Your email address is never shown to other Players.

4. Service Providers

We share data only with the providers needed to run the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging): stores your account and game data and delivers notifications. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Play and Apple App Store: process purchases and deliver push notifications on their platforms.

Data stored with Firebase may be processed on Google servers located outside your country. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. How Long We Keep Data

  • Account and game data: kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Friend chats: deleted automatically after 24 hours without new messages. Duel chats are deleted 15 minutes after the duel ends.
  • Friend requests: expire after 14 days if not answered.
  • Camera video: never stored.

6. Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the App:

Open AURIZE → System menu → Delete Account, then type DELETE to confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases:

  • Your account and login (your email can no longer sign in)
  • Your profile, progress, quest history, items and currency
  • Your profile picture
  • Your friends, blocks, chats and duel invites
  • Your saved location and push notification tokens

Deletion cannot be undone. Two kinds of record are kept after deletion: completed duel results, because they are also part of your opponent's history (your name in them is replaced with "Deleted Hunter"), and purchase records, which are needed for accounting and to prevent fraud. Reports made about chat messages may be kept for moderation.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

If you cannot access the App, email us from the address on your account (see Section 11) and we will delete your account within 30 days.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Permissions: you can allow or deny camera, location, motion, photos and notifications at any time in your device settings. If you deny camera or location, you can still log workouts manually.
  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your username and profile picture in the App, or ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: see Section 6.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Data Security

The System protects Player data with encrypted connections and Firebase security rules that restrict who can read and change each record. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

AURIZE is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover that a Player under 13 has provided data, it will be deleted immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The "Last Updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Please check this page from time to time.
